Taking Gift Cubes To The Next Dimension

Evolving beyond traditional maze cubes and video game gift boxes, the Present Reality VR Gift Cube combines 3D printing with embedded hardware to deliver an ultra-compact virtual reality puzzle box.

🚀 Project Concept

Created as an interactive gift vessel, this 3D-printed device houses dual displays and micro-controller hardware to simulate a miniature VR environment inside a portable cube.

⚡ Hardware Architecture

  • Brain ESP32 Microcontroller
  • Displays Dual TFT Screens
  • Graphics Engine TGX 3D Library
  • Enclosure Custom 3D Print
